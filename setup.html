Why "unsafe"? It's your own script, so Google hasn't reviewed it the way it reviews public apps. It only works inside your account: Gmail to read your Capitec statements, Sheets to keep your numbers, email to send you your link.

You're done when the log says Execution completed. A sheet called Jaeger data now sits in your Google Drive. That's where your data lives.

  1. Click Deploy (top right) → New deployment.

If something goes wrong

The editor says "Sorry, unable to open the file"

That happens when you're signed in to more than one Google account. Open a private window, sign in only to your Gmail and start again from step 1.

Run asks for permission again, or nothing happens

Click Review permissions and finish all three screens, ending with Allow. Then pick setup and press Run once more. Running it twice does no harm.

Step 6 says it couldn't reach my server

Check Who has access is Anyone (Deploy → Manage deployments → ✎, change it, Deploy). Also make sure you copied the Web app URL from the deployment box, not the editor's address bar.

The email didn't arrive

Look in Spam and Promotions for Your Jaeger link. Still nothing? Wait a minute and press Email me my link again. You can also paste the Web app URL straight into the app on your phone.

It opens in Safari instead of the Home Screen app

The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. Always open it from the icon, and paste your link there.

I lost my phone

On another connected device: Settings → Disconnect every device. Old links stop working at once. Then reconnect with your new link.

Jaeger reads your statements and notifications. It can't move money or log in to your bank. Shared as is.
