Your own money app

Jaeger runs on your own Google account. Your money stays in your own Google Sheet. Nobody else can see it, including whoever sent you this page.

TimeAbout 15 min
CostFree
You needGmail + Capitec

Part 1 · On a computer

Google's script editor is hard to use on a phone, so do this part on any laptop or desktop, signed in to your Gmail. You only do it once.

  1. Make a new project

    Go to script.google.com and sign in with your Gmail. A new project opens. Click Untitled project at the top and name it Jaeger.

  2. Show the settings file

    Click the ⚙ Project Settings gear on the left. Tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor. Then click the <> Editor icon to go back.

  3. Paste the three files

    For each file below: click Copy, click the file in the editor's list, select everything in it (Ctrl+A, or ⌘+A on a Mac) and paste (Ctrl+V).

    appsscript.jsonSettings: what the app may do
    Code.gsThe server
    EngineNew file: click + next to Files → HTML, name it Engine

    Press Ctrl+S to save. The editor turns the name Engine into Engine.html by itself.

  4. Run setup once

    In the bar at the top, pick setup in the function list and click ▷ Run. Google asks for permission:

    Review permissions → your Gmail → Advanced → Go to Jaeger (unsafe) → Allow

    "Unsafe" only means Google hasn't reviewed it, because it's your own script, not a public app. It needs Gmail to read your Capitec statements, Sheets to store your money and email to send you your link.

    It makes a Google Sheet called Baobab data in your Drive. That's where all your data lives.

  5. Put it online

    Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app. Set:

    Execute as: Me
    Who has access: Anyone

    Click Deploy, then Copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.

    "Anyone" lets your phone reach your server. It doesn't show your data to anyone: every request needs your private key, which only goes to your own Gmail.

  6. Get your link

    Paste the Web app URL here. Your server checks it, then emails your private link to your Gmail.

Part 2 · On your phone

  1. Install the app

    On your iPhone open Safari (not Chrome) and go to . Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.

  2. Connect it

    Open the email Your Jaeger link. Press and hold Open Jaeger and choose Copy. Open Jaeger from your Home Screen and tap Paste my link or address.

    A short welcome sets your name, icon, colour, payday and savings pockets.

  3. Bring in your money

    In the Capitec app, open your main account → Email statement, pick the last 3 to 6 months and send it to your Gmail. The app finds it by itself.

    Want every tap to show up live? In the app go to Settings → iPhone Shortcuts: three steps, once.

Good to know

  • Laptop too. In the app: Settings → Email my link, then open the email on your laptop.
  • Lost your phone? Settings → Disconnect every device. Old links stop working.
  • Updates to the app arrive by themselves. If the server ever needs an update, you paste the new Code.gs and choose Deploy → Manage deployments → ✎ → New version.
  • Your data is yours. Delete the Baobab data sheet and the script, and it's all gone.
Jaeger is a personal project shared as is. It reads your statements. It can't move money or log in to your bank.
